# CAUSALOS launch identity

## Style prompt
An open scientific instrument, printed on warm paper. Precise cobalt lines link hypotheses, interventions and evidence. Large statements use the same quiet serif register as the laboratory. Genuine application captures occupy the middle of the film. A thin annotation rail carries simulation labels. Motion explains the arrival of evidence and preserves reading time.

## Colors
- Paper `#f5f4ee`: background.
- Ink `#222a31`: statements and structure.
- Cobalt `#2846c9`: hypotheses, interventions and identity.
- Amber `#936628`: simulation and model-conditional warnings.
- Surface `#fcfbf7`: instrument panels.

## Typography
- Display: Iowan Old Style, with Palatino and Georgia fallbacks.
- Interface: system sans, using the host system's installed face.
- Labels: SFMono-Regular, with Consolas and monospace fallbacks.

## Motion
0.4–0.65 second entrances; restrained crossfades; slow camera movement on actual application captures. No continuous motion in data labels. Posterior quantities come from executed simulation state. The score includes soft original electronic tones and two tiny interaction accents.

## What not to do
- No invented numerical results or comparative superiority claims.
- No real-world verification claims for a simulated intervention.
- No abstract particles, fake terminal output or decorative charts.
- No paid API, copied music or voice narration.
- No unrelated visual redesign of the application.
