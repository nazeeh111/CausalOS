Text version and scientific limits

0:00 · Same observations. Different explanations. The v0.1.1 laboratory shows a causal question and competing structural models with a 50.0% prior probability for A → Y.

0:03.6 · Choose an experiment. The selected action sets A to 1, measures Y using eight samples, and costs two units. Its expected query information gain is 0.289 nats. Query gain and whole-model gain are distinct criteria.

0:08.5 · Follow the evidence. After three simulated interventions costing six units, P(A → Y) is 3.2% when rounded to the interface's precision. The B–Z orientation remains unresolved. These probabilities compare the supplied hypotheses; they do not rule out a missing explanation.

0:15 · No confirmation. No estimate. In the measured-confounding example, withdrawing the no-unmeasured-confounding confirmation makes the local application withhold the effect estimate. Checking a box cannot establish that an assumption is true.
